- 2026-10-18: simulation-based fitting of the whole training process (model_sim_design.md, rl4_agents, 015, 016), per user request.
  Recovery (40 mice x 8 generating agents) failed: per mouse the generating agent is selected 10-30% of the time (chance 12.5%), and
  pooled over mice bigger agents win even on data generated by the core agent (core ranked last). Diagnosis: the score compares one
  stochastic learning trajectory (the mouse, or a pseudo-mouse) with the MEAN of simulated runs under a binomial likelihood, which ignores
  run-to-run (path) variability of learning agents, so extra flexibility always pays. Results of 015/016 not interpretable until fixed.

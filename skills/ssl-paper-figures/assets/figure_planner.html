Bisi & Hamon Figure Planner

Bisi & Hamon figure planner

Lay out each figure on the 183 mm Nature grid and write what every panel shows. Claude reads this page and turns it into the figure spec.
Connecting…
Click an empty square to add a panel (square by default). Drag a panel anywhere to move it; its size never changes. Drag its corner to draw its size. Edges snap to the grid lines and to other panels' edges (blue line) when within 2 mm, otherwise to 1 mm steps; hold Alt to place freely. Exact mm values can be typed in the panel editor. With automatic letters on, letters follow reading order (left to right, top to bottom) after every add, move or delete, as in the built figure.
